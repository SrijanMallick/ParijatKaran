Profile

Parijat Karan

Advocate — Patna High Court & CAT, Patna

Parijat Karan is an Advocate practising before the Patna High Court and the Central Administrative Tribunal, Patna. With a focused practice in Service Law, Insolvency & Bankruptcy Law, and Commercial Law, he represents clients with diligence and a deep commitment to constitutional and procedural fairness.

His legal writing explores comparative constitutional jurisprudence, judicial review of legislative processes, and the rights of citizens under secular marriage laws — reflecting a thoughtful engagement with the evolving landscape of Indian and international law.
